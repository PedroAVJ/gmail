# Icon sources

The Gmail assets in `assets/`, `skills/gmail/assets/`, and
`skills/gmail-inbox-triage/assets/` are copied from the OpenAI Gmail plugin at
commit `11c74d6ba24d3a6d48f54a194cd00ef3beea18f9`. Its manifest declares MIT;
the exact declaration and corresponding license text are preserved in `licenses/`.

`skills/gmail-cli/assets/gmail-app-icon.png` is a byte-identical downstream
copy of the upstream `assets/gmail.png` at that commit. No separate app-store
artwork is included. `skills/gmail/assets/gmail.svg` duplicates the upstream
`gmail-small.svg` without modification.
